I'm Eric, and **Blackhaven Labs** is the name I use to document my projects.

I like building things, improving processes, and turning what I learn into something useful for others.

Much of my technical work grew out of process improvement: noticing inefficiencies or unexpected behavior and figuring out what would make things work better. That led into analytics, data engineering, data science, software development, architecture, and developer tooling. I enjoy moving between those areas, especially when a problem is best understood as part of a larger system.

## Projects & interests

The majority of my professional experience has lived in internal environments, but I've recently started using GitHub as a home for my projects.

Most of what you'll find here will be software and tooling, but it ranges pretty widely: privacy and developer utilities, Minecraft Bedrock projects, data and mapping work, automation, prototypes, experiments, and the occasional idea I took a little too far and turned into a project.

## Current work

### [Kalypt](https://github.com/BlackhavenLabs/Kalypt)

A local-first tool for finding and removing information you might not intend to share in a file, including metadata, hidden content, and credentials.

### [OpenFailure](https://github.com/BlackhavenLabs/openfailure)

An experimental project for studying how systems fail and finding recurring patterns across incidents from different fields.

### Minecraft Bedrock

I spend what is probably an unreasonable amount of time on the technical side of Minecraft Bedrock. It's a fun place to apply computer science and engineering ideas and build things other players can use, with most of my work centered on Storage Tech, quality-of-life features, technical information and diagnostics, and Bedrock scripting/add-on development.
